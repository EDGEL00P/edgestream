TV Guide

Channels that behave like real TV.

Bring a playlist or portal login and EdgeStream turns it into a proper lineup. Channel numbers follow real broadcast numbering for each country, so 101 on the remote is BBC One HD, every time.

  • Every channel resolved to its real name, logo, category and country
  • A guide merged from several sources, with catch-up where offered
  • Names that could mean two channels are left alone, never guessed

Sports

Press OK on a game. Watch the game.

Scores, fixtures, standings and odds for 40+ leagues, and every game already knows where it's on. EdgeStream checks, in order:

  1. Your pickSaved for that game or that team.
  2. The event channelThat exact match: kick-off time and competition both checked.
  3. The TV guideA channel whose listing names both teams at kick-off.
  4. The broadcasterThe network listed as carrying it.

On Demand

Your collections, on the big screen.

Curated collections with their own artwork, defined remotely and changed without an update. Press Play and EdgeStream finds the best copy from your own sources: your home media server first, then your provider, then your add-ons.

  • Continue Watching, My List and Up Next with a timed autoplay
  • Online subtitles, found automatically when you want them
  • Stream quality at a glance before you press play

Around the house

Built for the whole living room.

Phone remote

Scan a code and your phone becomes a remote: D-pad, typing and voice. Ask "what's on Fox League?" or "when do the Broncos play?"

Move to another TV

Copy providers, settings, profiles and your lists to a second TV with a six-digit code.

Weather and planner

Local weather and storm radar, plus a planner that reminds you before the game starts.

Security

Private by design.

Encrypted at rest

The whole database is encrypted, with its key held by the TV's secure hardware.

Sealed secrets

Logins and keys you type are sealed one by one, never stored in plain text.

End-to-end remote

The phone link is encrypted with a key that only travels inside the pairing code.

No account, no cloud

Everything runs on the TV. There is no EdgeStream server to sign in to.
